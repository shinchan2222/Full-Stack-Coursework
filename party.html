About The Banquet

What is this Event?

The Straw Hat Pirates have reached another island after an incredible adventure. To celebrate their journey, Captain Monkey D. Luffy has organized the biggest banquet in pirate history.

Everyone is welcome! Whether you are a pirate, a marine, a chef, a swordsman, or simply an anime fan, there is something for everyone.
